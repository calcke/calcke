calcke · guide

WHAT IT IS

A handheld mental-maths trainer. It shows a problem, you type your answer and press SUBMIT, and it checks and times you. It never shows the answer until you have committed yours; the result screen then shows you against the key and underlines the first digit that differs.

KEYBOARD

Digits and . as printed · Enter SUBMIT · Backspace DEL · M MODE · S SHIFT · arrow keys · Esc RESET.

SHIFT LAYER

Tap SHIFT, then a key within 4 seconds. Tap SHIFT again to cancel. The yellow text above a key is its SHIFT function.

SHIFT +Does
7 START/STOPStart from the menu or result screen; quit from a problem or memorise screen
8 FASTToggle auto-submit
9 REPORTShow the session report
4 PARAM+Increase the selected setting
5 PARAM−Decrease the selected setting
6 JUMPNext setting

MENU

  • MODE next mode
  • UP / DOWN (or .) pick a setting
  • LEFT / RIGHT change it
  • 0 toggle FAST
  • SUBMIT start (resets the session stats)

Press any key on the boot screen to reach the menu. Settings are remembered.

DURING A ROUND

  • Type digits (up to 22). DEL removes the last one.
  • SUBMIT checks your answer (needs at least one digit).
  • FAST on: auto-submits once you've typed as many characters as the key has.
  • Quit to the menu: MODE twice within 1.5 s, or SHIFT+7.

RESULT SCREEN

  • CORRECT exact match
  • CLOSE (SQ ROOT only) within 0.01%
  • WRONG anything else
  • SUBMIT next · MODE menu · SHIFT+9 report

HARDWARE

Display: 400 × 240 memory LCD over SPI (SCLK 18, SI 23, SCS 17, DISP 19, pump SD 22). Key matrix: rows GPIO 13, 14, 25, 26, 27; columns GPIO 32, 33, 4, 35. Power: 2×AAA or USB-C. SWD programming pads on the board.
